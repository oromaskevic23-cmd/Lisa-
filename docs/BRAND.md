## Display rules

- Maintain original square proportions and center the entire gold coin in promotional contexts.
- Preserve the faceted orange/cream fox face, golden eyes, black inner ears, black/gold circular framing and uppercase LISA wordmark.
- Avoid wallet-brand impersonation or claims of MetaMask affiliation, endorsement or partnership.
- Do not change the primary mark, colors or spelling without approval from the project owner.
- Source photography, designs and other contributors require permission, attribution and provenance where applicable.
